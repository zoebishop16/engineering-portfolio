## Project Overview

Built with a partner at **Cornell BigRed//Hacks 2022**, the oldest student-run, large-scale hackathon at Cornell University. Over three days, Cornell and non-Cornell students come together to build innovative projects.

Sustainability issues are having an exponentially growing negative impact on our planet. To highlight these issues and advocate for change, we created **A Sustainable World**, an app where users learn about sustainability through simple, fun mini-games.

![The three mini-worlds: Ocean, City, and Farm](/static/images/sustainable-world-worlds.jpg)

## Inspiration

We drew inspiration from *Dumb Ways to Die*, a popular app that originated as a Melbourne Metro Trains public service announcement about train safety. We wanted to create a similar experience for sustainability: simple, entertaining games that leave the player with something to remember.

## What It Does

The app features three mini-worlds — **Ocean World**, **City World**, and **Farm World** — each focused on a different area of sustainability. Every world has its own mini-game, such as saving a sea turtle by flicking plastic trash away, designed to be quick and enjoyable while weaving in education. Each world also includes a **Learn More** section with short facts, like how much CO₂ a passenger vehicle emits each year or how much recyclable waste actually gets recycled.

![Home screen, the Ocean World turtle game, and the City World "Learn More" facts](/static/images/sustainable-world-screens.jpg)

## How We Built It

- Designed the full UI/UX and interactive prototype in **Figma**, including screen-to-screen navigation and game interactions.
- Created background and illustration elements in **Canva**.

## Challenges and Takeaways

Neither of us had a design background, so the hackathon was a hands-on crash course in Figma and in thinking through the best user experience. We are especially proud of the visual design we produced, and we came away with a much stronger understanding of prototyping and user-centered design.
